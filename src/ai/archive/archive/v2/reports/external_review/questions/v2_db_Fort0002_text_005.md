## Question
How did the historical control of Rajgad Fort transition following the Treaty of Purandar in 1665?

## Options
A. Rajgad was retained by Shivaji as a core stronghold, while many other forts were surrendered to the Mughals
B. Rajgad was completely surrendered to the British East India Company as part of a trade concession
C. Rajgad was handed over to the Nizam of Hyderabad and converted into a coastal port
D. Rajgad was peacefully gifted to the Siddees of Janjira in exchange for naval support

**Correct Answer:** A

## Evidence
- **Source Evidence:** Historical context associated with the Treaty of Purandar (1665) indicates Shivaji surrendered 23 forts to the Mughals but retained 12, importantly including his capital Rajgad.
